# Project Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophical foundation of DR1FT. Every
architectural, design and implementation decision must align with these
principles.

## Why DR1FT Exists

Digital manipulation has become part of everyday life. Young people
encounter misinformation, extremist narratives, scams, cyberbullying,
AI-generated media and algorithmic influence before they receive
structured education about them.

DR1FT provides a safe, simulated social-media environment where these
mechanisms can be experienced, recognized and reflected upon.

## Core Philosophy

-   Learning by Experience
-   Reflection over Rewards
-   Competencies over Scores
-   Player Agency
-   Scientific Foundation

## Product Principles

-   Feed First
-   Content Driven
-   Engine Driven
-   AI First
-   Solo Developer Friendly

## Non Goals

DR1FT is not:

-   A traditional quiz app
-   A classic RPG
-   A multiplayer social network
-   A platform promoting extremist content

## AI Development Principles

-   Preserve architectural consistency.
-   Separate content from code.
-   Prefer reusable engines.
-   Avoid duplicated business logic.
-   Keep Phase 1 achievable by a solo developer.

## Summary

These principles are considered stable and should guide every future
decision.
