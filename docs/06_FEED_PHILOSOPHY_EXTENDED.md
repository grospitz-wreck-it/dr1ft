# Feed Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy of the DR1FT feed. The feed is not
merely a user interface---it is the primary environment where narrative,
learning, gameplay and algorithmic systems converge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Feed is the World

In DR1FT, the feed replaces the traditional game world.

Players do not travel through maps or levels. They navigate an evolving
stream of digital information that reflects their choices, interests and
behavior.

Everything meaningful begins in the feed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Living Information Space

The feed should feel alive.

It continuously changes through:

-   new posts
-   comments
-   reactions
-   direct messages
-   community activity
-   missions
-   educational interventions
-   algorithmic recommendations

The player experiences a believable digital ecosystem rather than static
content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content is the Environment

Posts are not decorations.

Every item in the feed has a purpose.

Examples include:

-   advancing a story
-   presenting manipulation
-   introducing characters
-   triggering missions
-   encouraging reflection
-   reinforcing competencies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Algorithm as Storyteller

The recommendation system shapes the player's experience.

It does not simply optimize engagement.

Instead, it creates meaningful learning opportunities by adapting the
feed according to previous interactions.

The algorithm becomes an invisible narrative engine.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Authenticity

The feed should resemble modern social platforms without imitating any
specific commercial service.

It must feel familiar while maintaining its own visual identity and
ethical standards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Reflection Through Interaction

Every interaction is educational data.

Examples:

-   liking
-   sharing
-   saving
-   reporting
-   commenting
-   ignoring
-   following
-   blocking

The educational meaning emerges from patterns over time rather than
isolated actions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Feed as Narrative Canvas

Stories unfold naturally inside the feed.

Narrative elements include:

-   posts
-   conversations
-   creator profiles
-   trending topics
-   community discussions
-   breaking events

Traditional cutscenes should remain the exception.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Feed as Learning Engine

The feed supports:

-   observation
-   critical evaluation
-   emotional awareness
-   source comparison
-   pattern recognition
-   reflective decision-making

Learning is embedded within ordinary platform use.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Design Principles

-   Feed First
-   Content Before Chrome
-   Authentic Before Artificial
-   Context Before Explanation
-   Discovery Before Instruction
-   Reflection Before Reward

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

A successful feed enables players to:

-   feel immersed,
-   discover organically,
-   question information,
-   understand algorithmic influence,
-   build lasting digital resilience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Summary

The feed is the heart of DR1FT.

Narrative, gameplay, AI, education and progression all emerge from
interactions within a believable, evolving stream of digital content.
