# Gameplay Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines what gameplay means in DR1FT and establishes the
principles that guide every mechanic, interaction and future feature.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Gameplay Vision

DR1FT is not built around combat, resource management or reflexes.

Its gameplay is the process of observing, interpreting, deciding and
reflecting within a simulated digital ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Feed is the Game

The social feed is both the primary interface and the primary gameplay
space.

Players do not leave the feed to "play."

Interacting with the feed is the gameplay.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decisions Create the Experience

Every action matters.

Examples include:

-   liking a post
-   ignoring content
-   opening comments
-   following profiles
-   reporting misinformation
-   replying to messages
-   joining communities
-   completing missions

Each interaction influences future experiences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Meaningful Consequences

Consequences should be educational rather than punitive.

The player learns by observing how decisions affect:

-   recommendations
-   trust
-   narrative progression
-   relationships
-   competency development

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Gameplay Loop

1.  Discover content.
2.  Observe carefully.
3.  Make a decision.
4.  Experience consequences.
5.  Reflect on outcomes.
6.  Build competencies.
7.  Continue exploring.

This loop forms the core of every scenario.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Narrative Integration

Gameplay and storytelling are inseparable.

Stories emerge through digital interactions instead of scripted
cutscenes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Learning Through Systems

Educational outcomes arise from interacting with systems---not from
reading long explanations.

Algorithms, conversations and missions become learning tools.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# No Artificial Grinding

Players should never repeat meaningless tasks simply to unlock progress.

Advancement comes from exploration, reflection and understanding.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Player Agency

DR1FT should present choices rather than prescribe behavior.

Different decisions may lead to different learning experiences while
supporting the same educational goals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Replayability

Scenarios remain replayable because:

-   feeds evolve differently,
-   recommendations change,
-   characters react differently,
-   missions appear in different contexts,
-   player choices influence progression.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

Successful gameplay enables players to:

-   remain curious,
-   think critically,
-   recognize manipulation,
-   reflect on consequences,
-   transfer learning to real-world digital environments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Summary

Gameplay in DR1FT is the continuous interaction between the player, the
simulated platform and the educational systems beneath it.

Learning is not separate from gameplay.

Learning is the gameplay.
