# AI-First Philosophy

## Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence is integrated into
DR1FT. AI is a foundational capability that supports development,
content creation and personalized learning, while always remaining
subordinate to the educational mission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI-First Vision

DR1FT is designed as an AI-first platform.

Artificial Intelligence is not an isolated feature. It is a supporting
layer that improves scalability, productivity and educational quality
across the entire project.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI in Development

AI accelerates software development by assisting with:

-   architecture design
-   coding
-   documentation
-   testing
-   refactoring
-   asset generation
-   localization
-   quality assurance

Human oversight remains mandatory for all important decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI in the Product

Within DR1FT, AI supports:

-   adaptive learning paths
-   personalized reflections
-   educational feedback
-   dynamic NPC dialogue
-   scenario variations
-   content recommendations
-   accessibility features

AI should enrich learning without replacing critical thinking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Educational Responsibility

AI never decides what users should believe.

Its purpose is to encourage:

-   questioning
-   comparison
-   evidence-based reasoning
-   reflection
-   informed decision-making

Educational intent always has priority over automation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Ethical Principles

Every AI system in DR1FT should be:

-   transparent
-   explainable where appropriate
-   privacy-conscious
-   fair
-   bias-aware
-   aligned with educational goals

Manipulative AI behavior is explicitly prohibited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Human-in-the-Loop

Critical educational content should always remain reviewable by humans.

AI suggestions support authors---they do not replace them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Creation

AI may assist in creating:

-   posts
-   comments
-   NPC conversations
-   missions
-   learning prompts
-   localization
-   placeholder assets

Final content should undergo editorial review before release.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI as an Assistant

AI acts as:

-   mentor
-   facilitator
-   creative assistant
-   productivity tool

AI is never presented as an unquestionable authority.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Long-Term Vision

As AI capabilities evolve, DR1FT should integrate new technologies
responsibly while preserving its educational philosophy and maintaining
user trust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

Successful AI integration should:

-   reduce development effort,
-   improve educational quality,
-   personalize learning,
-   increase accessibility,
-   preserve transparency,
-   strengthen digital resilience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Summary

AI is a foundational enabler of DR1FT.

Technology serves education---not the other way around.

Every AI capability should empower users to become more independent,
reflective and resilient in digital environments.
