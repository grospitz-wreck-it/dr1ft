# Core Vision

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of DR1FT and establishes the
immutable product vision for all future development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision Statement

DR1FT is an AI-first educational platform that simulates a modern social
media ecosystem where users learn to recognize manipulation through
authentic experiences rather than passive instruction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision Principles

## Feed is the World

The simulated feed is not a feature.

It is the entire game world.

Every interaction begins and ends inside the feed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Experience Before Instruction

Players should discover patterns themselves before receiving
explanations.

Reflection always follows interaction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Digital Resilience

The objective is not to teach isolated facts.

The objective is to build transferable competencies that help users
navigate digital environments safely.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scenario Independence

Every topic is implemented as content.

Examples include:

-   Online Radicalization
-   Deepfakes
-   Fake News
-   Love Scams
-   Cyberbullying
-   AI Manipulation
-   Financial Fraud

The platform remains identical.

Only the content changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Engine First

Reusable engines always have priority over scenario-specific
implementations.

Every new feature should strengthen the platform instead of solving only
one scenario.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Human Centered AI

Artificial Intelligence accelerates development and supports learning
experiences.

Educational responsibility always remains with the designed learning
objectives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Long-Term Product Vision

DR1FT evolves from a single educational application into a scalable
media-literacy platform capable of supporting schools, research
institutions, educators and future learning scenarios through reusable
content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

A successful DR1FT experience enables users to:

-   Question information critically.
-   Recognize manipulation patterns.
-   Reflect on emotional influence.
-   Make informed decisions.
-   Transfer these competencies to real digital environments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Anti Vision

DR1FT will never become:

-   A conventional social network.
-   A content recommendation platform.
-   A competitive grinding game.
-   A platform that rewards engagement over learning.
-   A system that sensationalizes extremist content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Summary

The vision remains stable throughout the lifetime of the project.
Technology, engines and content may evolve, but the core vision should
remain unchanged.
